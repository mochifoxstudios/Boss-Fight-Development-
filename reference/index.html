// About This Game
◇ How this game was made
This game was developed with the assistance of large language models and generative AI tools, used for graphic assets, concept art, narrative and lore drafting, design review, and code. Every AI-generated image was edited and composited by hand in Adobe Photoshop before use; none ships in its raw generated form. Not all assets are AI-generated — the interface icons, navigation glyphs, city buildings and prestige-tree symbols are drawn programmatically as vector graphics, and all music and sound effects are synthesised procedurally at runtime by hand-written audio code. All lore, mechanics, art and systems are pre-authored and static: no generative model produces game content at runtime, and the game makes no live model calls of any kind. The optional accessibility narration uses the operating system's built-in speech synthesis to read existing on-screen text aloud.

 THE BASICS

                    

                        
Just a Clicker Game is an incremental game about harvesting energy from a simulation that may or may not be aware of you.

                        
    
                            
  • Press ACQUIRE (or Space) to generate energy manually. Each click gives energy based on your click power and active combo.
    • 
                            
  • Use energy to buy generators in the Shop — these produce energy automatically, every second, even while offline.
    • 
                            
  • Generators compound: buying more of the same type scales output dramatically.
    • 
                            
  • The ★ NEXT UP badge in the Shop marks the single best next purchase for your current income level.
    • 
                            
  • Watch the Objective strip below the button — it always shows your next milestone and how close you are.
    • 
                            
  • The EPS counter (Energy Per Second) is your most important number — everything you do should aim to grow it.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                

                    
⌬ COMBO SYSTEM

                    

                        
Clicking rapidly builds a Combo Chain that multiplies both your clicks and your passive income. A progress bar at the top shows combo level.

                        
    
                            
  • CHAIN ×2 — click twice within ~1.5 s
    • 
                            
  • FRENZY ×3 — keep the chain going
    • 
                            
  • OVERDRIVE ×4 — sustain clicking for full benefit
    • 
                            
  • SURGE / CRITICAL / MAXIMUM — unlocked later with LP upgrades; multiplicatively more powerful
    • 
                        

                        
Stop clicking for ~2 seconds and the chain resets. Tip: even slow combos give passive EPS bonuses — worth maintaining even while AFK.

                        
The Combo Overdrive Indicator at the top glows at max tier. Watch for CRITICAL HIT flashes — bonus-multiplied single clicks that can give hundreds of times normal output.

                    

                


                

                    
⬡ LEVEL POINTS & PRESTIGE

                    

                        
Legacy Points (LP) are earned every ~150 clicks and by certain world events. They never reset on prestige.

                        
    
                            
  • Open PRESTIGE on the nav bar → the Neural Tree to spend LP on permanent upgrades: click power, city grid bonuses, combo tiers, walk speed, and more.
    • 
                            
  • Prestige resets your energy and generators but gives a permanent global EPS multiplier — every successive run is exponentially faster.
    • 
                            
  • You keep: LP, Achievements, People Points, Fragments, Core Binding auras, Museum artifacts.
    • 
                            
  • You lose: raw energy, purchased generators, shop upgrades, active spells.
    • 
                            
  • The PRESTIGE window shows the price of the next collapse. It rises steeply with every run.
    • 
                            
  • Prestige unlocks new panels: Corruption Compiler, Core Binding, and higher-tier buildings in the city.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                

                    
▸ SHOP

                    

                        
    
                            
  • Buy quantity — ×1, ×10 and ×100 buy exactly that many (the card shows the price of all of them); MAX buys as many as you can afford.
    • 
                            
  • Click Items — add flat power per manual click (Fingertip Interface, Relay Node…). Stack as many as you can afford.
    • 
                            
  • Auto Generators — passive energy per second. Each type has a different base EPS; buying multiples of the same type multiplies output.
    • 
                            
  • PP Upgrades — spend People Points here on permanent city and income bonuses that survive prestige.
    • 
                            
  •  Power Routing — tap the dropdown to expand three bandwidth sliders: Energy (EPS multiplier), City (PP multiplier + adjacency amp), Anomaly (bird spawn rate). Total must not exceed 100%. Over-allocating the City sector above 80% doubles all adjacency bonuses.
    • 
                            
  • Numerical Abstraction — toggles a ×100 multiplier in exchange for losing exact readout. High-risk, high-reward.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                

                    
🏙 CITY NEXUS

                    

                        
The City is an 8×8 grid where you manually place buildings to earn People Points (PP) and adjacency bonuses that boost EPS.

                        
    
                            
  • Open the City panel → click a building card → click an empty tile to place it. Right-click a tile to lock or unlock it. Click an existing building to demolish it: it costs nothing, and nothing comes back.
    • 
                            
  • Walkers spawn and wander the grid; each time one visits a building it generates PP. More buildings = more walkers.
    • 
                            
  • Adjacency pairs: Tech+Tech → +28% EPS per pair; Industry+Industry → +18% EPS; Residential+Civic → +25% PP. Pack matching types for huge bonuses.
    • 
                            
  • City Diversity: each unique building category (Tech, Industry, Nature, Civic, Residential) you use adds +4% global EPS, up to +20% at 5 categories.
    • 
                            
  • PP accumulates permanently. Every 50 PP = +1% global EPS. Spend PP in the City Upgrades sub-tab for permanent boosts.
    • 
                            
  • Watch City Awareness %. It rises when instability is high (>60%) and falls slowly on its own. At 100%, citizens rebel: PP gain stops for 60 s. Keep it low with Propaganda Machine upgrades or Compiler spells.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                

                    
⚗ CORRUPTION COMPILER

                    

                        
What it is for: the Compiler turns instability into short bursts of power. Every spell adds to the meter or spends it, so the number you usually fight becomes something you pay with. It opens after your first Prestige. Each spell holds a few charges, and a spent charge comes back on its own.

                        
    
                            
  • OVERCLOCK: ×2 EPS for 45 s. Adds 25% instability; blocked above 75%. 2 charges, one back every 20 min.
    • 
                            
  • SURGE PROTOCOL: ×5 EPS for 20 s. Adds 45% instability; blocked above 50%. 1 charge, back after 45 min.
    • 
                            
  • FLUX DRAIN: removes 30 instability at once. Needs at least 5%. 3 charges, one back every 15 min.
    • 
                            
  • ECHO BURST: calls a Lucky Pulse orb now. Adds 15% instability; blocked above 85%. 2 charges, one back every 30 min.
    • 
                            
  • FRAGMENT FORGE: turns instability into 2 to 8 Fragments (more at higher instability) and lowers City Awareness by 5%. Adds 20% instability; needs at least 20%. 2 charges, one back every 25 min.
    • 
                            
  • NEXUS PULSE: ×3 People Points and walker speed for 30 s. Adds 10% instability; blocked above 90%, or when City Awareness is 60% or more. 1 charge, back after 35 min.
    • 
                        

                        
With Core Binding: a bound aura changes one or two spells, and each aura card says how. FLUX CORE, for one, makes OVERCLOCK ×2.5. Museum artifacts make OVERCLOCK, SURGE and NEXUS PULSE last longer.

                    

                


                

                    
◈ CORE BINDING

                    

                        
What it is for: Core Binding is where Fragments become permanent power. You open up to three sockets, at your 1st, 3rd and 6th Prestige, and bind one aura into each. A bound aura keeps working through every Prestige, and each one also changes one or two Compiler spells.

                        
    
                            
  • FLUX CORE (15 Fragments): +10% EPS. OVERCLOCK gives ×2.5 instead of ×2.
    • 
                            
  • IMPACT SHELL (12): +8% click power. While OVERCLOCK runs, click power ×2; while SURGE runs, ×3.
    • 
                            
  • DAMPENER CORE (20): instability grows 15% slower. FLUX DRAIN removes 45 instead of 30, and for the next 60 s instability grows 25% slower.
    • 
                            
  • TEMPORAL WEAVE (25): +5% LP earned. ECHO BURST also gives 1 Fragment.
    • 
                            
  • CASCADE RESONANCE (30): +0.5% EPS for each different item you own, counting up to 50 (+25%). ECHO BURST calls 2 Lucky Pulses; OVERCLOCK doubles this bonus while it runs.
    • 
                            
  • CITY NEXUS BIND (35): +10% EPS, and your city's adjacency bonus +25%. NEXUS PULSE lasts 45 s instead of 30 and gives ×4 People Points instead of ×3.
    • 
                            
  • FRAGMENT TIDE (28): +0.05% EPS for each Fragment you hold, up to +20%. FRAGMENT FORGE makes 50% more Fragments.
    • 
                        

                        
Museum artifacts strengthen every aura but the Dampener. Unbinding or replacing an aura destroys it, and the Fragments it cost are gone. The preview shows the change before you commit.

                    

                


                

                    
✺ MEGA PROJECTS

                    

                        
Late-game universe-scale construction goals unlocked through prestige. Each project requires massive energy and Data investments but grants permanent multipliers. Projects in the [INTERCONNECT] category bridge multiple game systems.

                        
Main chain: Orbital Collector → Dyson Nexus → Reality Compiler → Entropy Reverser → Omega Beacon → Reality Singularity → The End

                        
    
                            
  • Orbital Collector — +5% global production. Teaches the project system.
    • 
                            
  • Dyson Nexus — ×2 all city output. Unlocks Signal Lattice side-branch.
    • 
                            
  • Reality Compiler — +50% all output. Passively generates +1 DATA/s (×1.6 under Surveillance policy).
    • 
                            
  • Entropy Reverser — 25% of energy spent on shop purchases refunded as DATA automatically.
    • 
                            
  • Omega Beacon — ×3 to all prestige bonuses permanently.
    • 
                            
  • Reality Singularity — ×2 all production. Compiler spells recharge at double speed. Unlocks Thermal Sinks.
    • 
                            
  • The End — ×5 global output forever. Terminal configuration achievement.

Side branches:

⋎ ANOMALY BIRDS

                    

                        
Birds fly across the screen at random intervals. Click them to claim a bonus. They disappear after a few seconds — act fast!

                        
    
                            
  • Common (white/blue) — small energy or fragment bonuses.
    • 
                            
  • Rare (gold) — larger rewards, special effects, temporary EPS boosts.
    • 
                            
  • Legendary (glowing rainbow) — massive one-time bonuses: prestige multipliers, huge fragment grants, instability purges.
    • 
                            
  • Fragments, combos, and city Anomaly Labs all increase spawn rate (up to +60% from fragments).
    • 
                            
  • The Anomaly Power Routing sector in the Shop also controls spawn frequency directly.
    • 
                            
  • High combo tiers grant a bird spawn rate bonus. Prestige runs speed up the interval further.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                

                    
⚠ INSTABILITY

                    

                        
Rapid clicking raises Instability. It decays on its own at ~0.6%/s when you stop clicking.

                        
    
                            
  • 5%+ — Flux Drain spell becomes available.
    • 
                            
  • 20%+ — Fragment Forge spell available.
    • 
                            
  • 40–49% — prime window for Controlled Overload (×4.2 EPS for 45 s).
    • 
                            
  • 45%+ — Glitch Market opens for instability ↔ energy/fragment trades.
    • 
                            
  • 60%+ — UI begins to distort; EPS display may flicker; City Awareness grows faster.
    • 
                            
  • 75%+ — shop item names scramble; Overclock spell is blocked.
    • 
                            
  • 90–99% — Redline Bonus: ×10 EPS (dangerous window, act fast).
    • 
                            
  • 100% — Reality Fracture cascade. Major consequences — may trigger lore events or reality rewrites.
    • 
                        

                        
City Awareness >70% bleeds back into instability. Keep both under control using DAMPENER aura and FLUX DRAIN spells.

                    

                


                

                    
◈ FRAGMENTS & SECONDARY RESOURCES

                    

                        
    
                            
  • Fragments (◈) — earned from Controlled Overload, world events, rare birds, Fragment Forge spells, and Simulation Slots. Spend on Core Binding auras, City upgrades, and Fragment Upgrades.
    • 
                            
  • Data — generated by walkers entering Tech buildings (+0.5/visit) and Tech districts (0.05/s per slot). Spend on Data Cache (+5%), Data Matrix (+12%), Data Nexus (+25%+PP×2) for compounding EPS boosts.
    • 
                            
  • Slag — rare by-product of Reality Fractures and certain lore events. Used in advanced upgrades and black market items.
    • 
                            
  • People Points (PP) — earned by city walkers. Every 50 PP = +1% global EPS, uncapped. Also spent on PP Tree upgrades for combo tiers, walk speed, and more.
    • 
                            
  • Holding more Fragments increases anomaly bird spawn rate by up to +60%.
    •

⊛ SPECIAL ENTITIES

    •

    • 
                            
  • Offline income does NOT include combo bonuses, bird clicks, or manual clicks — only passive generator EPS.
